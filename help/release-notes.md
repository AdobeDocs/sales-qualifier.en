# Sales Qualifier Release Notes

**08-17-2026**

[!DNL Sales Qualifier] is now available as a stand-alone application. It was initially released as part of Adobe Journey Optimizer B2B.

This release includes:

* Prospect and account prioritization with AI-generated activity summaries and signal-based scoring
* Goal-driven Engagement Plans with AI-proposed cadences and drafted emails
* A unified task queue for phone calls, LinkedIn InMails, and email reviews
* Automatic meeting booking through calendar integration
* A Knowledge Center for grounding AI outreach in your own playbook materials
* AI Chat for natural-language questions based on your CRM, engagement, and Knowledge Center data
* Email and meeting-booking performance reports
* Browser and email plugins for access within your CRM or Outlook
* Support for Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics 365 integrations
